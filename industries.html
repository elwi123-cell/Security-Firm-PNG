Plan a suitable arrangement

Questions to clarify before engagement

Security requirements should be defined for the actual site and checked with the provider before any agreement is made.

Understand the risks

Describe your site layout, operating hours, entry points, valuable assets, people movement and known concerns.

Define the scope

Agree on duties, staffing, supervision, communication, incident escalation, reporting and boundaries of authority.

Verify credentials

Ask for current security-company licensing and guard permits, and verify them with the Security Industries Authority before contracting.

PNG regulatory information

Verify licensing before you engage a provider

The Security Industries Authority (SIA) regulates private security companies and security guard permits in Papua New Guinea. Check current records directly with the Authority.

🏛️

Security Industries Authority

SIA guidance says service receivers should verify that a security company is licensed and that guards hold permits. Do not rely on historic listings as proof of current compliance.
