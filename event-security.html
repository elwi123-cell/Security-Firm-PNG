Event security considerations

Build an event-specific security brief

Use these topics to explain what your event requires. They are planning and enquiry areas, not a confirmation that every service is currently offered by Kuima.

🚪

Entry & Access Management

Discuss entry and exit points, guest check-in, ticket or invitation checks, restricted areas and procedures for managing unauthorised access.

👥

Crowd & Guest Safety

Describe the expected attendance, venue layout, crowd flow, high-traffic areas and any foreseeable congestion or safety concerns.

🎤

Stage & VIP Areas

Identify performers, speakers, officials, backstage spaces or other areas requiring controlled access and clear movement arrangements.

🚗

Vehicle & Parking Areas

Explain vehicle entry points, parking arrangements, loading zones, pedestrian routes and any need to separate vehicles from guests.

📻

Communication & Escalation

Agree in advance on event contacts, supervisor communications, incident reporting and how concerns are escalated to event organisers and emergency services.

🧭

Venue & Perimeter Awareness

Share venue maps, boundaries, lighting conditions, access limitations and areas where additional observation may be required.

🩹

Emergency Readiness

Coordinate with venue management on emergency exits, evacuation routes, assembly points and the role of authorised emergency responders.

📋

Event Risk Briefing

Outline event timing, programme changes, expected attendance, known sensitivities and any previous incidents relevant to planning.

🤝

Organiser Coordination

Set out who makes operational decisions, who receives incident updates and how security duties fit with venue staff and event organisers.

Confirm service availability, staffing, equipment, venue suitability and all relevant licensing directly with Kuima Security Services Ltd before making commitments or advertising security coverage.

Before the event

Information to prepare

A concise event brief helps a provider assess the request and identify questions that must be resolved before an agreement.

Event details

Provide the event name and type, venue address, date, setup and pack-down times, expected attendance and whether the event is indoors or outdoors.

Scope & responsibilities

Discuss the number and placement of personnel, duty boundaries, supervision, guest handling, reporting and coordination with venue management.

Compliance & written terms

Verify current provider licensing and guard permits with PNG’s Security Industries Authority, and document costs, duties, insurance and cancellation arrangements.

How to proceed

From event enquiry to agreed arrangements

Use a clear process to avoid assumptions and establish who is responsible for each part of the plan.

Describe

Share the venue, event date and hours, expected attendance, programme and main concerns.

Discuss

Ask Kuima to confirm whether it can support the request, subject to its current capabilities and availability.

Verify

Check current licences, guard permits, references, insurance, staffing and venue-specific requirements.

Document

Agree on duties, contacts, incident escalation, emergency coordination, pricing and cancellation terms in writing.

PNG regulatory guidance

Verify credentials before engagement

The Security Industries Authority regulates private security providers in Papua New Guinea. Its published guidance advises clients to verify that the company is licensed and the guards it employs have permits.

✓

Check provider licensing

Ask for current licensing details and verify them with the Security Industries Authority rather than relying on old lists or promotional material.
