Site-specific planning

Mining and resource-site considerations

Every project has its own operating rules and risk profile. The following are common topics to discuss during an enquiry, not a representation that Kuima currently supplies every listed service.

🚧

Site access & visitor control

Clarify access points, worker and visitor verification, contractor movements, induction requirements and who is authorised to approve entry.

🛠️

Equipment & asset protection

Identify high-value equipment, stores, fuel areas, materials and any site-specific procedures for reporting suspicious activity or loss.

👷

Worker and contractor safety

Align any security duties with site induction, emergency arrangements, occupational health and safety requirements and the site manager’s authority.

📋

Incident reporting

Agree on incident categories, escalation contacts, record keeping, confidentiality and the process for notifying authorised client representatives.

🤝

Community interface

Discuss local protocols, landowner and community engagement arrangements, respectful communication and referral pathways for concerns.

🌙

Operating schedules

Explain site location, roster, operating hours, shift handovers, communications coverage and any travel or mobilisation constraints.

The appropriate scope must be agreed with the client and checked against current company capability, legal requirements, site rules and written contract terms.

From enquiry to agreement

A clear process for discussing your site

Use these steps to prepare a useful initial conversation with Kuima.

Describe the site

Share the project location, site type, operating context and the main security concerns.

Define requirements

Outline access arrangements, operating hours, expected duties, reporting lines and staffing expectations.

Verify capability

Request current licensing details, guard permits, relevant references, insurance information and evidence of any required training.

Agree in writing

Confirm scope, costs, supervision, responsibilities, escalation procedures, duration and performance reviews before work begins.

PNG mining safety context

Security must complement mine safety

Private security duties should support—not replace—the mine operator’s statutory safety responsibilities, emergency plans or authorised site procedures.

🏛️

Mineral Resources Authority

The MRA’s Mines Inspectorate describes its role as promoting mine safety, conducting inspections and investigations, and helping protect mine workers from hazards and risks.
