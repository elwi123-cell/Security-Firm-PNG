Privacy & site security

Publish responsibly.

Before uploading an image, confirm that the company is authorised to publish it and that any people shown have given appropriate consent. Avoid visible ID cards, personal phone numbers, vehicle registration details, access codes, control-room screens, site maps and other sensitive information.

Do not identify a client, project, facility or security arrangement unless disclosure has been approved. Captions should be factual and should not imply a client endorsement or active contract without confirmation.
