Information you provide

What information may be involved?

The exact information handled depends on how you contact the company and the nature of your request. This page does not claim that Kuima collects every category listed below.

✉️

Contact enquiries

If you email or call the company, you may choose to provide details such as your name, contact information, organisation and the content of your enquiry.

🛡️

Service requests

Requests for security services or quotations may include business, site, scheduling or operational details needed to understand the request. Avoid sending sensitive site information unless the recipient is authorised.

🧾

Recruitment enquiries

If you apply for work or ask about recruitment, you may provide employment-related details. Send identification documents or other sensitive records only through a channel the company has confirmed as appropriate.

This is a general description of information a person may voluntarily provide when making an enquiry, not a verified inventory of the company’s systems or records.

Responsible handling

Keep information safe and relevant.

Good privacy practice starts with collecting and sharing only what is needed for a legitimate purpose.

Purpose and relevance

When contacting the company, explain your request clearly and avoid including unrelated personal details or confidential information belonging to another person.

Limited sharing

Do not publish private correspondence, identity documents, security arrangements or contact details without permission or a lawful reason.

Secure communication

Use care when sending attachments, check the recipient address, and confirm the appropriate channel before sharing sensitive operational or personal records.

Your choices

Ask about your information.

If you have sent personal information to Kuima Security Services Ltd and have a question about it, contact the company using the details on this page. Describe the enquiry and the information concerned, but do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive details.

You may also choose not to include optional details in an initial enquiry. However, the company may need enough information to understand and respond to a service request.

Any request relating to access, correction, retention or deletion of information will need to be considered by the company in light of the records held, applicable requirements and the circumstances of the request. This webpage does not promise a particular outcome or timeframe.
