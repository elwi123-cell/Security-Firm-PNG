About Kuima

A Papua New Guinean security company based in Lae

Kuima Security Services Ltd identifies itself as 100% nationally owned and lists its head office on Independence Drive, Malabu, Lae, Morobe Province.

Public reporting in March 2021 described Kuima as a Lae-based company with experience on mine sites, and reported a three-year joint-venture agreement with Golpu-Wafi Security Services for mine security. This is historical reporting, not confirmation of a current contract.
