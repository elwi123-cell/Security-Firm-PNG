Prepare your details

What prospective suppliers should provide.

Use this checklist to prepare a clear introduction. Only provide documents that are relevant and safe to share through the chosen contact channel.

🏢

Business identity

Registered business or trading name, business structure, principal place of business, and the name and role of an authorised contact person.

🧾

Registration and tax details

Where applicable, have your IPA business registration details, IRC tax identification or other relevant documents available for verification. Do not send original documents unless requested through an appropriate process.

📦

Products or services

Describe what you supply, service coverage, delivery capacity, relevant experience, indicative pricing and any applicable standards or certifications.

This checklist is general guidance, not a confirmed official Kuima supplier policy or an exhaustive list of mandatory requirements.

Suggested next steps

From enquiry to possible consideration.

These steps explain a sensible enquiry pathway and do not represent a guaranteed internal procurement timeline.

Introduce your business

Complete the enquiry form with accurate contact details and a short description of your offering.

Prepare supporting records

Keep current business, tax, product, safety and capability documents ready if requested.

Company review

Kuima may request clarification or documents. An enquiry alone does not constitute supplier approval.

Written confirmation

Wait for formal communication before representing yourself as an approved supplier or commencing work.

Responsible supplier engagement

Keep information accurate and secure.

Provide truthful details and disclose any relevant limitations, conflicts of interest or subcontracting arrangements when requested. Do not offer bribes, gifts intended to influence a decision, or misleading claims about past work.

Do not send passwords, bank login credentials or unnecessary personal information. Bank and payment details should only be shared through a verified and appropriate channel when genuinely required.
