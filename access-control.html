Planning topics

What to discuss for gate and entry management

These are common planning areas for a site-access discussion, not a guarantee that every capability is available from Kuima.

🪪

Visitor identification

Discuss how visitors are expected to identify themselves, who authorises entry and whether temporary visitor records or badges are required.

🚚

Vehicle entry

Define vehicle categories, delivery procedures, authorised parking or unloading areas, and the site contact responsible for approving exceptions.

📋

Entry and exit records

Agree which information may be recorded, why it is needed, who may access it, how it is stored and when records should be retained or disposed of.

🔑

Authorisation checks

Set clear rules for employee, contractor and visitor access, including how permissions are checked and what to do when authorisation is unclear.

☎️

Escalation procedures

Identify the designated supervisor and site representatives to contact for denied entry, unexpected deliveries, disputes or safety concerns.

🦺

Safety and site rules

Explain relevant induction, personal protective equipment, restricted areas, emergency routes and site-specific safety requirements before access is granted.

Do not submit access credentials, detailed security layouts, alarm codes, identity documents or sensitive site procedures through an initial website enquiry.

Before service begins

Set responsibilities in writing

A clear written scope helps prevent confusion at entry points and supports consistent, accountable procedures.

Define who may enter

Document categories of authorised persons and vehicles, approval responsibilities, visitor rules and any site-specific exceptions.

Protect personal information

Collect only necessary information, tell people why records are taken, restrict access to records and agree on secure handling.

Confirm authority and capability

Before engagement, check current company licensing and guard permits with PNG’s Security Industries Authority and request relevant insurance and references.
