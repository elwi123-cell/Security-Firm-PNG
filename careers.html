Careers in security and support

Security work calls for reliability, sound judgement, respect for people and property, clear communication and the ability to follow lawful instructions and site procedures. Depending on business needs, opportunities in a security company may involve guard duties, supervision, operations support, administration, driving, maintenance or other specialist functions.

The role types above are examples of positions that may exist in the industry, not confirmation that Kuima is currently recruiting for each role. Contact the company to verify current opportunities, duty stations, employment conditions and application requirements.

Before you submit

Prepare a clear application

Unless a vacancy notice specifies otherwise, you can prepare the following information for a recruitment enquiry.

Prepare your CV

Include your current contact details, relevant work history, skills and references where appropriate.

List qualifications

Be ready to provide copies of relevant certificates, licences, training records or other credentials requested for the role.

Check role requirements

Confirm the location, duties, working hours, eligibility criteria and any role-specific permit or fitness requirements.

Apply through a verified channel

Email the company or contact the office to confirm the correct recipient and submission process before sending documents.

Professional expectations

What responsible security work involves

Requirements vary by role and assignment. These are general professional principles, not a published Kuima-specific selection checklist.

Reliability

Attend as agreed, communicate promptly and follow approved procedures.

Integrity

Act honestly, handle information appropriately and report facts accurately.

Respect

Treat clients, colleagues, visitors and members of the public fairly and professionally.

Safety awareness

Recognise hazards, follow site instructions and escalate concerns through the proper channels.

Communication

Give clear handovers, follow reporting lines and document incidents appropriately.

Role-specific compliance

Maintain the permits, training and qualifications required for the position, where applicable.
