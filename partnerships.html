A clear approach

How to present your proposal.

Use these suggested steps to make your initial enquiry useful and easy to assess.

Introduce your organisation

Provide your legal or trading name, location, contact person and a brief description of your work.

Explain the opportunity

Describe the proposed collaboration, service scope, intended locations, target outcomes and anticipated timing.

Outline contributions

Clarify each party's proposed responsibilities, resources, qualifications, costs, risks and dependencies.

Agree in writing

Wait for formal review and a written agreement signed by authorised representatives before relying on any commitment.

Responsible collaboration

Build the relationship on clarity.

Partnership proposals should be accurate, practical and transparent. Clearly identify the parties, intended objectives, contributions, commercial assumptions and any relevant licences, insurances or certifications.

Protect confidential information, avoid conflicts of interest, and do not make commitments on behalf of Kuima without explicit authority. Any commercial arrangement should be reviewed and documented by the parties before work begins.
