Site-specific planning

Construction and infrastructure site considerations

These are common issues to discuss during an enquiry, not a representation that Kuima currently supplies each listed service. The final scope should be based on project requirements, site rules, risk review and a written agreement.

🚧

Perimeter & access points

Map fences, temporary gates, entry routes, visitor check-in, authorised access lists and procedures for damaged or unsecured boundaries.

🚜

Plant, tools & equipment

Identify high-value machinery, tools, parked vehicles, equipment keys, storage compounds and agreed checks for movement in and out of the site.

🧱

Materials & deliveries

Discuss delivery schedules, loading and unloading zones, materials storage, supplier access and the records needed to investigate discrepancies.

👷

Workers & contractors

Clarify site induction requirements, visitor identification, subcontractor authorisation, sign-in and sign-out, and who can approve access to restricted areas.

🌙

After-hours site checks

Discuss weekend and overnight arrangements, lighting, unattended areas, plant parking, emergency contacts and how concerns should be escalated.

📋

Incident coordination

Agree reporting routes for trespass, suspected theft, property damage, hazards or emergencies, including when site management, police or emergency services must be contacted.

Security arrangements should complement—not replace—site safety procedures, inductions, hazard controls and emergency plans. Security personnel should not be asked to perform construction or high-risk safety tasks outside their training and agreed duties.

From enquiry to agreement

A clear process for your project

Prepare a concise overview of the work site, project stage and security concerns.

Describe the project

Share the site location, project type and stage, operating hours, access points, contractor activity and known concerns.

Define site duties

Discuss requested duties, coverage times, perimeter and gate procedures, reporting lines, shift handovers and site-specific instructions.

Verify credentials & induction

Request current company licensing, guard permits, relevant references and insurance information; coordinate site induction and required PPE rules with the principal contractor.

Agree in writing

Confirm responsibilities, pricing, supervision, incident escalation, access authority, service hours, contract duration and review arrangements.

Responsible security

Site security must work with safety and compliance

Security arrangements should support site rules, protect people and property, respect lawful rights and work alongside the project’s health and safety management arrangements.

🏛️

Check company licensing

The Security Industries Authority regulates private security companies in Papua New Guinea. Ask the Authority to confirm a provider’s current licence status before engagement.

Verify guard permits

SIA advises clients to check that security companies are licensed and their employed guards hold the required permits. Historical listings or directory entries do not establish current compliance.
