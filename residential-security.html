Property-specific planning

Residential security planning considerations

These are common issues to discuss during an enquiry, not a representation that Kuima currently supplies each listed service. The final scope should be based on the property, residents’ needs and a written agreement.

🏡

Home access points

Review doors, gates, windows, garages and other entry points. Agree which household members, visitors or service providers may be authorised to enter.

🔑

Keys & access control

Clarify who holds keys, how spare keys are managed, how access codes are protected and what to do when keys are lost or a resident moves out.

🏢

Apartments & shared entrances

Discuss entry procedures for residents, visitors and contractors, common areas, lift or stairwell access and responsibilities shared by property management.

💡

Lighting & visibility

Identify poorly lit paths, parking areas, entrances and shared spaces. Consider practical improvements and keep exits and access routes unobstructed.

📞

Emergency contacts

Keep contact details current for residents, property managers and emergency services. Agree how suspicious activity, damage or urgent concerns should be reported.

🌙

Absences & after-hours routines

Discuss safe routines when residents are away, who may check the property, how deliveries are handled and how concerns should be escalated.

Security measures should be lawful, proportionate, respectful of residents and visitors, protect privacy, and be consistent with property rules and applicable PNG requirements.

From enquiry to agreement

A clear process for residential properties

Prepare a concise description of the property and the safety concerns you want to address.

Describe the property

Share the location, property type, occupancy, access points, shared areas and any known concerns.

Define the requirements

Discuss requested duties, coverage times, visitor procedures, reporting contacts and any property-specific instructions.

Verify credentials

Request current company licensing, guard permits, relevant references, insurance information and any training records appropriate to the work.

Agree in writing

Confirm responsibilities, pricing, supervision, incident escalation, service hours, contract duration and review arrangements.

Responsible security

Professional conduct and compliance matter

Security arrangements should support residents’ safety while respecting lawful rights, privacy and the peaceful use of homes and shared residential spaces.

🏛️

Check company licensing

The Security Industries Authority regulates private security companies in Papua New Guinea. Ask the Authority to confirm a provider’s current licence status before engagement.
