A practical approach

How to raise a complaint.

The steps below are suggested guidance for submitting a concern. They are not a claim that a formally adopted company complaints policy or response deadline has been independently verified.

Explain the issue

Describe the event or service concern in plain language, including the date, approximate time and location where relevant.

Share relevant facts

Include any reference number, names or role descriptions you know, and supporting information that is appropriate to share.

Tell us the outcome sought

Explain what you would like reviewed, clarified or improved. Keep the request specific and realistic.

Allow follow-up

Provide a safe contact method. The company may need further information to understand and assess the matter.

Helpful information

What to include in your message.

Only provide information that is relevant to the concern and that you are authorised to share.

📅

Date, time and location

Identify when and where the issue occurred as accurately as possible. If the matter concerns a client site, avoid disclosing sensitive site access or security arrangements.

📋

What happened

Describe the actions, service, communication or incident that caused concern. Separate what you directly observed from what you believe may have happened.

🔒

Privacy and confidentiality

Do not include passwords, financial account details, unnecessary personal information, or confidential operational plans. Ask for a suitable channel if sensitive evidence needs to be provided.

Urgent safety matters

Immediate danger needs immediate action.

If someone is in immediate danger or a serious incident is unfolding, do not rely on a website form or email for an urgent response. Contact the appropriate emergency services or responsible site emergency contact, and use the company hotline where it is relevant to the security service involved.

For non-emergency complaints, use the contact details on this page. A submission does not itself confirm receipt, investigation, a particular response time or a specific outcome.
