Learning and readiness

Professional development for security work

Security assignments can require personnel to understand site instructions, communicate clearly, observe and report incidents, manage access responsibly and respond appropriately to hazards or emergencies. Training needs depend on the duties, client site, applicable rules and each person's existing qualifications.

This page describes common training topics in the security sector; it is not a confirmed list of courses currently offered by Kuima. Contact the company to verify whether a specific course is available, who delivers it, the entry requirements and whether a certificate or permit is included.

Guiding principle
“Your security is our business.”
Competence, conduct and safety matter on every assignment.
Possible learning areas

Topics to ask about

These are examples of subject areas that may be relevant to security personnel. They are not a promise that Kuima currently runs these courses.

🛡️

Security duties & procedures

Post orders, patrol awareness, observation, incident reporting, shift handovers and following lawful site instructions.

🚪

Access control

Visitor handling, authorisation checks, entry records, gate procedures and escalation of unusual or unauthorised activity.

🚨

Emergency awareness

Understanding alarms, evacuation arrangements, emergency communications and the limits of a security officer's role.

🦺

Health, safety & environment

Recognising hazards, using required protective equipment, reporting unsafe conditions and following site safety rules.

🤝

Communication & conduct

Professional interaction, respectful conflict de-escalation, confidentiality and clear communication with supervisors and clients.

📋

Supervision & reporting

Where relevant, team coordination, accurate records, operational reporting and consistent application of approved procedures.

Course content, delivery method, duration, prerequisites and assessment must be confirmed directly with the provider.

Before enrolling

Questions to confirm first

Request clear details before attending or paying for any training activity.

Confirm the course

Ask for the exact course title, learning outcomes, schedule, venue and intended participant group.

Check the provider

Ask who will deliver the training and whether the course requires an approved or recognised provider.

Understand costs

Confirm fees, what they include, payment terms and any refund or cancellation conditions in writing.

Verify certification

Ask whether assessment is required, what document is issued and whether it meets the requirement for your intended role.

Professional standards

Learning should support responsible practice

Training complements supervision and site procedures; it does not replace permits, legal requirements or client-specific instructions.

Stay within your role

Understand your authority and escalate matters that require a supervisor or emergency service.

Follow site rules

Learn and apply the approved instructions, reporting lines and emergency arrangements for each assignment.

Keep records accurate

Record observations and incidents factually, promptly and through the correct reporting channel.

Prioritise safety

Use required protective measures and do not undertake tasks for which you are not trained or authorised.

Respect people

Communicate professionally and treat colleagues, visitors and the public with dignity.

Refresh knowledge

Ask supervisors about changes to procedures, hazards, site conditions and training requirements.
