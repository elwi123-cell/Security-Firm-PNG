Site-specific planning

Commercial and retail security considerations

These are common issues to discuss during an enquiry, not a representation that Kuima currently supplies each listed service. The final scope should be based on a site assessment and written agreement.

🛍️

Retail floor & customer areas

Discuss visible security presence, customer assistance, staff safety, store opening and closing routines, and a respectful approach to incidents.

📦

Stockrooms & inventory

Identify stock storage, delivery bays, high-value merchandise, keys and authorised access. Agree how suspected losses or irregularities should be recorded and escalated.

🏢

Office & building access

Clarify visitor sign-in, contractor access, staff identification, reception procedures, restricted areas and who can authorise entry.

🚚

Deliveries & loading zones

Plan vehicle movements, delivery time windows, driver checks, loading-area access and communication with warehouse or store supervisors.

📋

Incident reporting

Agree incident categories, escalation contacts, report timing, evidence handling, confidentiality and when police or emergency services should be contacted.

🌙

After-hours premises

Discuss closing checks, keys, alarms, lighting, staff departures, call-out arrangements and how authorised contacts are notified of an incident.

Security measures should be lawful, proportionate, respectful of customers and employees, and consistent with the client’s procedures and applicable PNG requirements.

From enquiry to agreement

A clear process for your business

Prepare a concise description of the premises and what you need protected.

Describe the premises

Share the address or area, business type, operating hours, public access patterns and any known concerns.

Define the scope

Discuss requested duties, coverage times, access procedures, reporting lines, staffing and site-specific instructions.

Verify credentials

Request current company licensing, guard permits, relevant references, insurance information and any training records appropriate to the work.

Agree in writing

Confirm responsibilities, pricing, supervision, incident escalation, service hours, contract duration and review arrangements.

Responsible security

Professional conduct and compliance matter

Security arrangements should help people feel safe while respecting lawful rights, privacy and the normal operation of a business.

🏛️

Check company licensing

The Security Industries Authority regulates private security companies in Papua New Guinea. Ask the Authority to confirm a provider’s current licence status before engagement.
