Putting procedures into practice

From written policy to daily operations.

Effective procedures should be appropriate to the assignment and clearly understood by the people expected to follow them.

Define requirements

Identify site risks, client expectations, applicable laws and responsibilities.

Document procedures

Record roles, reporting lines, escalation contacts and required records.

Brief personnel

Explain relevant procedures and confirm personnel understand their duties.

Review & improve

Check performance, investigate concerns and update procedures when required.

Client and personnel guidance

Confirm the current approved documents.

Before a contract or deployment begins, clients should agree on the service scope, site instructions, emergency contacts, incident-notification timeframes, reporting format, supervision arrangements and complaint process in writing.

Personnel should seek clarification from their supervisor if an instruction is unclear or appears unsafe or unlawful. Concerns should be reported through the appropriate company or client channel.

This webpage is an informational overview. It is not a substitute for a signed contract, site-specific post orders, legal advice or Kuima’s current controlled policy documents.
