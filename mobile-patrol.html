Patrol planning

A patrol plan shaped around your property

Mobile patrols are commonly considered by organisations seeking periodic visible checks of property, access points and selected assets. The appropriate approach depends on the location, operating hours, access arrangements, identified risks and what the client needs documented after each visit.

This page explains topics prospective clients can discuss with Kuima. Patrol planning topics

What to discuss before arranging patrols

Use these prompts to describe your needs. They are planning topics, not a promise that each capability is available from Kuima.

🏢

Property and perimeter checks

Identify the property boundaries, authorised access points and areas that a patrol, if agreed, would be expected to observe.

🕒

Patrol schedule

Discuss preferred days, time windows, frequency, planned versus randomised visits and any restrictions on site access.

🚗

Vehicle and access arrangements

Confirm whether vehicle-based checks are suitable, where vehicles may enter or park, and how patrol staff would identify themselves.

📋

Observation and reports

Agree on the information to record after each visit, such as arrival time, observations, exceptions and the authorised recipient of reports.

☎️

Escalation and contact

Set out who should be contacted if an issue is observed, what counts as urgent, and when police or emergency services should be contacted.

🔑

Keys and site instructions

Document any approved key custody, alarm instructions, confidentiality expectations and procedures for access to restricted areas.

Do not send sensitive site layouts, alarm codes, keys, vulnerability details or confidential security procedures through an initial website enquiry.

Client preparation

Agree the details before service begins

A clear written scope helps both parties understand what patrol coverage includes and what it does not include.

Describe the location

Provide the general site location, property type, operating hours and a contact person who can discuss requirements securely.

Define the service

Confirm patrol frequency, visit duration, boundaries, reporting format, incident escalation, response limits and any exclusions in writing.

Verify authorisations

Before engagement, check current company licensing and guard permits with the PNG Security Industries Authority and request relevant insurance and references.

A practical process

From enquiry to a patrol arrangement

A suggested discussion sequence for prospective clients.

Enquire

Share the general location, type of property and the outcome you want from patrol visits.

Discuss

Review possible coverage, schedule, access arrangements, communication and reporting expectations.

Verify

Confirm current service availability, licensing, permitted personnel, insurance and references.

Document

Agree in writing on price, duties, limitations, reporting, escalation and contract terms before work starts.
