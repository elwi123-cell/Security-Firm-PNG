Partnership principles

What a responsible security relationship should establish

Whether discussing a new client assignment or a community-based partnership, expectations should be clear from the outset.

Clear responsibilities

Define the services, sites, operating hours, points of contact, reporting requirements and escalation arrangements in writing.

Local participation

Where relevant, discuss workforce needs, community liaison, role-specific training and how local participation can be managed responsibly.

Current due diligence

Confirm current company and guard licensing, insurance, references, safety processes, pricing and contract terms before work begins.

Working together

A practical path to a new engagement

Use a transparent process to determine whether the proposed arrangement is appropriate for both parties.

Make contact

Share your organisation, location, project context and the reason for the enquiry.

Define the need

Discuss scope, operating conditions, expected outcomes, timelines and responsibilities.

Complete checks

Review current credentials, references, insurance, procurement requirements and risks.

Document terms

Record deliverables, supervision, reporting, costs, escalation and review procedures in writing.
